3. Hardware & Thermal Safety Warning

🔥
High Heat Hazard:
Puffco Peak Pro and Proxy vaporizers contain high-temperature ceramic and quartz heating chambers capable of reaching temperatures in excess of 600°F (315°C), as well as internal high-discharge lithium-ion batteries.

By utilizing puffco-ble to send heating, boost, or profile commands to your hardware:

  • You acknowledge that you are operating physical thermal hardware that presents burn, fire, and battery hazards if operated unattended or in the presence of flammable materials.
  • You agree to never initiate heating sessions when the device is unattended, unmonitored, or out of line of sight.
  • You accept that misuse, excessive heat boosts, or dry firings may damage heating coils, atomizers, or invalidate manufacturer warranties.
  • The authors and contributors of puffco-ble accept zero liability for any thermal damage, hardware breakdown, burns, property damage, or personal injury resulting from the use or malfunction of this software.
