3. Hardware & Safety Warning

🔥
Puffco vaporizers contain high-temperature heating chambers capable of exceeding 600°F (315°C) and internal lithium-ion batteries.

By using puffco-py to send heating commands:

  • You acknowledge operating thermal hardware with burn, fire, and battery hazards.
  • You agree to never initiate heating sessions when the device is unattended.
  • You accept that misuse may damage hardware or void warranties.
  • The authors accept zero liability for any resulting damage or injury.
